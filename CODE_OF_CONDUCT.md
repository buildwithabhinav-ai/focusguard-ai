# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in FocusGuard AI to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that keep the community open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps the community includes:

- being kind and patient with other people,
- respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences,
- giving feedback that is specific and constructive, and accepting it the same way,
- owning mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from them,
- focusing on what is best for the community and not only for ourselves.

Behavior that is not acceptable includes:

- sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind,
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks,
- public or private harassment,
- publishing someone else's private information, such as a physical or e-mail address, without their permission,
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities of maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take fair, appropriate action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain the reason when they do.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions. It also applies when someone is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at [support.focusguardai@gmail.com](mailto:support.focusguardai@gmail.com). All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and security will be respected.

Maintainers will follow these steps when deciding the consequences of a violation:

1. Correction: a private, written warning that explains the violation and why the behavior was inappropriate, sometimes with a request for a public apology.
2. Warning: a warning with consequences for continued behavior, including a period of no interaction with the people involved.
3. Temporary ban: a temporary ban from interaction in project spaces for a set period.
4. Permanent ban: a permanent ban from any interaction with the project community, for a pattern of violations or serious harassment.
